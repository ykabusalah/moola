Common Questions

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device, in Apple's encrypted local storage. It never touches a server we run — because we don't run any.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Open Settings and tap the export option. You'll get a CSV file you can share, save, or copy anywhere you like. You can also turn on backup reminders from Settings.

What happens if I delete the app?

All your moola data is deleted with it. There's no cloud copy. Export a CSV first if you want to keep your records.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. App lock is off by default. Turn it on under Settings → Security and set a 4–6 digit PIN.

How do I set a recurring expense?

When adding an expense, tap the recurring option and pick weekly, monthly, or yearly.

I'm getting duplicate reminders / no reminders.

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → moola to confirm permission is granted. Inside moola, toggle the daily reminder off and back on to reschedule it.

I forgot my PIN.

Because your PIN is stored only on your device with no recovery method, the only way to regain access is to delete the app and reinstall — which erases your data. Export CSV backups regularly if this concerns you.

Do you have an Android version?

Not yet.
