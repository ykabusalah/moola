moola

quiet your coins

moola is a small, private expense tracker for iPhone.

No accounts. No cloud. No servers. Your data lives on your device and nowhere else. Everything about the app is designed around that single idea.

What it does

  • Log expenses in seconds, with optional notes
  • Recurring expenses (weekly, monthly, yearly)
  • Views by day, week, month, or year
  • Optional daily reminders to log
  • Optional backup reminders to export
  • Face ID or PIN protection (off by default)
  • Light and dark mode
  • CSV export via share sheet
  • Fully offline

Why

Most expense apps want your bank login or sync your history to servers you'll never see. moola doesn't. If it left your device, it wouldn't be yours anymore.
